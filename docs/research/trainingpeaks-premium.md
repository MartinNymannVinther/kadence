## 4. Prioritised shortlist

1. **Forward projection of form to the next A/B race** (S–M). This is the one piece of TP's Premium planning core Kadence lacks, and it answers the taper question. Show it; never make it a target.
2. **Multi-week block templates applied from a date** (M). It saves the volunteer coach the most time across seasons, fits "template" and `createGroupSessions`, and runs guardrail checks on apply. This is TP's Dynamic Plans and plan library without a marketplace.
3. **Process goals on the calendar, with DCU's 30 strength sessions as the first automatic one** (S–M). It makes ADR 0013's "goals are process first" concrete. Coach, rider and parents see the same checklist.
4. **Season bests, own vs own** (S–M). It replaces TP's Peak Performances and StackUp with a youth-safe version: no percentiles, no W/kg under 19.
5. **Planned vs done per interval** (M). This is TP's Analyze 360 aimed at the coach's real question, built from the structure Kadence already stores.
6. **Indoor as a ride subtype, plus recurring availability** (S each). Small fixes that TP users waited years for. Availability also feeds the week draft.
7. **A weekly digest email for riders and guardians, deterministic and opt-in** (M plus an ADR). TP's weekly snapshot, pointed at parents, which is Kadence's differentiator. It needs a decision on email.
8. **Estimated threshold as a coach-accepted proposal** (M). It spares juniors frequent max tests. Do it after one winter's data, as the RPE calibration is also meant to wait (roadmap).

## 5. Explicitly NOT to copy

- **StackUp and percentile comparisons.** A leaderboard by another name, with W/kg and weight required. Conflicts with ADR 0013.
- **Fueling Insights and anything about calories or macros.** Nutrition is out in v1, it needs weight, and it carries eating-disorder risk for teenagers.
- **TP's form labels ("Going Hard", "Overloading", "Optimal") and target-fitness or ramp-rate planning in the ATP.** These are adult performance framings. Kadence uses DCU hours and corridors, and its own words.
- **The names TSS, CTL, ATL, TSB, NP, IF, and WKO's mFTP, FRC, Pmax and "anaerobic battery".** Trademarks or branding, so use Kadence's own words.
- **Messaging and 1:1 chat.** One open conversation (ADR 0012); direct messages are on the not-built list.
- **Tiering: Premium-only planning, per-athlete fees, coupon-waived setup fees.** Dogma 1.
- **Marketplace, Payments, Coach Match.** Payment is out in v1, and the product is not a storefront.
- **TP Virtual, and Oura/WHOOP HRV integrations.** Out of scope, US data flows, and HRV has low value for this age group.
- **Weather as a hard dependency.** If ever built, it must be an optional EU adapter that fails quietly.
- **Notifications that push "new PR!"** Push notifications are not built in v1, and results-first framing goes against process-first.
