# ADR 0012: One open conversation, no private messages

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-09-27

## Context

TrainingPeaks added chat in 2026 for adults only. Youth sport's
safeguarding practice is that an adult coach and a minor do not talk in
channels no one else can see.

## Decision

Comments sit on a planned session, an activity or a day, and belong to
the rider. Everyone who may read the rider's training reads the thread:
the coaches, the rider and the rider's confirmed guardians (unless the
rider stops sharing training). Coaches and the rider write, each under
their own name; an author edits their own words and a coach may take a
comment down. There are no direct messages.

## Trade-offs accepted

- **No private word.** A rider who needs to tell the coach something
  personal does it in person or by phone. That is deliberate.
- **Parents read, do not write.** A parent who wants to talk to the coach
  does so outside Kadence; the thread stays about the training.
